TV & Movie icon options

Two-column comparison (TV / Movie). Goal: a TV icon instantly recognizable in modern culture, distinct from a generic monitor, while fitting current style (white glyphs on app blue backgrounds, clear at small size).

TV options

Custom/simple (filled, current style)

Current (monitor-like)
tv-current
Existing icon: screen with stand/base, reads a bit like a PC monitor.
Flatscreen with feet
tv-feet
Wide screen body with two small feet; more living-room TV feel, no monitor neck.
TV with antenna (custom)
tv-antenna
Classic antenna silhouette makes it unmistakably a TV; check clarity at small size.
Modern pedestal base
tv-pedestal
Wider screen with a center neck/pedestal base, distinct from a monitor stand.
Retro/boxy TV
tv-retro-boxy
Chunky rounded TV body with a base strip; bold silhouette, recognizable as a TV.

External glyphs shown for recognition/style reference with license/source links.
